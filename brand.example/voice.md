# Brand voice (EXAMPLE, fictional creator "Huda Example")

Used as-is in the caption, critique and brief prompts. Replace it with your own voice guide.

## Voice
- Casual Saudi dialect, warm and direct, like advice to a friend who just graduated. Short sentences.
- Say the useful thing in the first line; no «السلام عليكم» intro, no long setup.
- Speak from what the creator actually did; never invent what she felt or learned (mark it ✍️ for her to fill).

## English tech terms
- Keep tool names in English (ChatGPT, Claude, LinkedIn, Excel, CV) and put the Arabic term once next to the English one when it helps search: «الذكاء الاصطناعي (AI)», «السيرة الذاتية (CV)».

## Emoji
- At most 3 per caption. Never 🚀 💰 🚨.

## Never say
- Anything not in facts.yaml about the creator. The traps in facts.yaml (2nd place, never "winner"; the internship dates; no ticket counts; no accuracy percentage for Receipt Reader).
- Guaranteed jobs, salaries or income; fear claims about AI taking jobs.
- Engagement bait (comment X if…, tag 3 friends, like if…).
- Paid promotion or third-party ads (Saudi Mawthooq rules).

## Privacy
- Never name teammates, managers or friends without their OK. Blur real names and numbers on screen.
